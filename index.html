Strategy Overview

NexyCapitals uses a rules-based swing trading strategy focused on high-quality, liquid S&P 500 stocks. The core idea: buy stocks in established uptrends that are temporarily pulling back to support — then ride the recovery. No discretion. Every entry and exit is defined by objective criteria.

Stock Universe

S&P 500 large-cap stocks with average daily volume above 1 million shares. Excludes stocks below $20 or with wide spreads. This ensures liquidity and reliable IBKR execution.

Setup Criteria (Entry Signal)

Uptrend confirmed: 50-day MA is above 200-day MA
Pullback to support: Price has pulled back to the 20-day MA or a prior support level (typically 5–15% off recent high)
Momentum cooling: RSI on daily chart between 35–50 (oversold relative to trend, not broken)
Volume pattern: Pullback on declining volume; entry day shows average or above-average volume
Entry
Buy at market open the day after setup confirms, or limit at prior day's close.
Stop Loss
Hard stop 7–8% below entry. Placed immediately upon fill.
Profit Target
Minimum 2:1 R/R. Trail stop on positions up >15%.
Hold Time
3–10 trading days. Exit early if thesis invalidated.
02

Risk Framework

Risk is checked first — every time. Hard limits are non-negotiable and enforced automatically by the bot.

Per-Trade Risk
1–2%
Max portfolio loss if stop is hit
Daily Loss Limit
3%

15%

4–6

Market Filter

No new entries when VIX is above 30 (high volatility regime).

No new entries when S&P 500 is below its 200-day MA (bear market filter).

Existing positions are managed normally even when filter is active.

03

Technical Architecture

code
Trading Bot
Python 3.12 + ib_insync
Connects to IBKR TWS or Gateway. Scans stocks, detects setups, executes orders, manages positions, enforces risk. Writes all state to Firestore via Firebase Admin SDK. Deployed on Google Cloud Run.
storage
State Layer
Google Cloud Firestore
Central truth store. Bot writes; portal reads. Real-time listeners keep portal in sync. Collections: /system/status, /positions, /orders, /trades, /backtests, /risk/state, /logs, /finance/account, /todos.
web
Observation Layer
NexyCapitals HQ Portal
Single-page HTML app on Firebase Hosting. Reads Firestore in real-time. Authenticated via Firebase Auth (single user). Writes system control signals back to Firestore (pause, stop).
04

AI Component (NEX)

NEX is not a trading agent. NEX does not place orders, manage positions, or override risk rules. NEX is a system intelligence layer — it reads Firestore state and answers questions about what the system is doing, why it made a decision, and what the current risk looks like.

What NEX does
Answers questions about current system state
Explains why a trade was taken or blocked
Summarises performance from trade log
Later: scores trade setups (advisory only)
What NEX does NOT do
Execute or cancel orders
Override risk limits
Access external market data
Make autonomous decisions affecting capital

06

Testing Protocol

1

Backtesting

Run strategy rules against IBKR historical data (2020–2025 minimum). Measure return, Sharpe ratio, max drawdown, win rate. Results saved to /backtests in Firestore and visible in Backtest Lab.

2

Paper Trading

Deploy bot to Cloud Run connected to IBKR paper account. Trade with live data, simulated orders, for minimum 30 trading days. Validate portal data accuracy, risk limits, and edge case handling.

3

Live Trading (Small Size)

Switch to live IBKR account with minimum position sizes ($500–$1,000 per trade) for 30 days. Compare to paper and backtest results. Scale only after consistent behaviour is confirmed.
