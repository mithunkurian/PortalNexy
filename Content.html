01Executive Summary
Building a lean, AI-operated SaaS from Stockholm
Nexy is a Kaizen-philosophy project management platform where an agentic AI handles the entire back-office — marketing, legal, finance, sales, and engineering — while the founder acts as chairman: approve or reject. The goal is not rapid growth, but learning how a real business works, reaching operational breakeven in 24 months, and building a sustainable side income.
The global project management software market was valued at USD 9.6 billion in 2026 and is projected to reach USD 32 billion by 2036, growing at a 12.9% CAGR. The AI-native segment specifically is forecast to grow at 15.7% CAGR through 2034. Nexy enters this market with a differentiated positioning — not as another feature-rich enterprise tool, but as a lean, opinionated platform built on the Kaizen philosophy of continuous small improvement, powered end-to-end by agentic AI.
The company is registered in Sweden, fully GDPR-compliant from day one, and targets individual professionals and small teams across Europe. Revenue is projected to begin in Month 9 via a freemium gate on AI features, reaching breakeven at approximately Month 24 with 50–70 paying users.

04Product & Service
What Nexy does and how it works
Nexy is a web-based project management platform that combines a Kaizen-philosophy task system, a Kanban board, document management, and an AI agent layer into a single coherent workspace. 05AI Agent Strategy
The AI brain that runs the company
Nexy is unique in that agentic AI operates not only inside the product (for users) but also behind the company itself. Claude Sonnet serves as the primary intelligence layer, with lighter models handling high-volume tasks. The founder acts as chairman — all agent-proposed actions require human approval before execution.

11Risk Analysis
Known risks and mitigation strategies
High
Market saturation
Notion, Asana, ClickUp dominate. Mitigation: differentiate on Kaizen philosophy + AI-native approach. Don't compete on features — compete on identity.
High
Founder bandwidth
Building a SaaS alongside a full-time engineering job is demanding. Mitigation: AI agents handle operational tasks. Founder focuses on product decisions only. Set a hard limit of 10 hrs/week.
Medium
AI API cost spikes
Unexpected usage growth could spike Claude API costs. Mitigation: rate limiting, usage caps, fallback to cheaper Haiku model for simple tasks. Budget alerts via agent.
Medium
Slow user acquisition
Free product with no marketing budget may struggle to reach 100 users. Mitigation: build-in-public storytelling is the marketing strategy. The AI-operated company narrative is the product launch.
Low
GDPR / regulatory risk
EU regulations evolving fast (EU AI Act, CSRD). Mitigation: AI agent monitors regulatory news weekly. All data EU-hosted. Agent flags policy updates proactively.
Low
Anthropic API dependency
Full reliance on Claude API. Mitigation: architect agent layer to be model-agnostic. Could switch to GPT-4o or Gemini if needed. No vendor lock-in at code level.
